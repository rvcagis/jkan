  Last Update (mm-dd-yyyy): <strong>08-07-2025<br><br>RVCA Regulated
  Flood</strong> represents the 100-year Floodplain Mapping extent (including a 15m allowance) within
  the Section 28 - Development, Interference with Wetlands and Alterations to
  Shorelines and Watercourses Regulation (Ontario Regulation 41/24). The 100-year flood has a 1:100 return period, or a 1-in-100 or 1% chance in any given year. Please see the associated <i>Regulation Limit Approved</i>
  dataset.
